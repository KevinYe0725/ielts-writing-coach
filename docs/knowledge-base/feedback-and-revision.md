# Feedback and revision rules

## Feedback is a process, not a comment dump

A useful feedback unit has five parts:

1. **Evidence:** quote the learner's exact words.
2. **Diagnosis:** name the real problem in plain Chinese.
3. **Impact:** explain what meaning, naturalness, organisation, or criterion is affected.
4. **Action:** tell the learner exactly what to change or practise.
5. **Check:** define what a successful new answer would demonstrate.

Bad: “表达不够高级，多用复杂句。”

Better: “`which makes children can develop` 同时使用了 `make + object + bare infinitive` 和情态动词结构。保留一个结构：`which helps children develop...`。下一题要在新主题中独立写出同类结果句。”

## Limit the load

- Start with the two or three issues with the highest score and transfer value.
- Group repeated occurrences under one decision rule.
- Do not provide ten near-identical corrections and call that depth.
- Separate “must fix”, “more natural”, and “optional polish”.

## Require learner uptake

Reading a correction is exposure. Revising the sentence shows the learner can use feedback with the original context visible. Independent generation in another context is stronger applied evidence. Delayed closed-book writing and cross-topic use are separate tests.

Therefore the workflow is:

`detailed correction → focused teaching → timed independent paper → delayed rewrite → different-topic transfer`

### What each stage owns

- **Detailed correction report:** preserve the original essay, compare original and corrected wording, expose small grammar/spelling/word-form leaks, explain paragraph logic, and give a transferable rule.
- **Focused teaching:** teach the selected ability through a decision rule, knowledge cards, an expression or thinking toolkit, a worked example, and two quick checks. It is learning time, not test time.
- **Timed paper:** test whether the learner can now recognise, repair, generate, and integrate the ability independently. It must not introduce hidden requirements or teach while marking.

Never use the paper to compensate for a thin report or missing teaching. If the learner cannot tell what ability is being practised before the timer begins, the package is invalid.

Do not label a target mastered after a same-sentence correction.

## Model answers

- Do not show a complete essay before the learner's delayed rewrite.
- After independent work, show one improved answer targeted to the missed requirement.
- Explain the decision that makes it better; do not imply the learner must reproduce the wording.
- Prefer multiple natural possibilities for collocation or information perspective when one fixed answer would be misleading.

## Failure handling

- An AI failure retries evaluation against the immutable answer, not the learner attempt.
- A missed question is saved as needing work and never traps navigation.
- A blank answer is `not_scorable`, not a language failure or system exception.
- If evidence remains insufficient at 60 minutes, end the paper and schedule a shorter recovery activity.
