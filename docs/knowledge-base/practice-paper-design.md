# Complete practice paper design

## Purpose

The paper converts one diagnosed weakness into active output. It is not another diagnostic report and it is not an eight-step tutorial.

It starts only after a focused teaching module has explained the same ability target. The teaching module and paper form one package: identical target, different job. Teaching may show reasoning and worked examples; the paper must require independent work and keep feedback locked until submission.

## Progression and time

| Questions |   Time | Learner action                                    |
| --------: | -----: | ------------------------------------------------- |
|       1–2 | 10 min | distinguish the target and explain the decision   |
|       3–4 | 14 min | repair flawed language while preserving meaning   |
|       5–6 | 16 min | generate original answers in different topics     |
|       7–8 | 20 min | use the ability in IELTS-style integrated writing |

All eight questions are visible from the start. Feedback, answers, and adaptations remain locked until the complete paper is submitted.

## One-place instruction rule

Each question's Chinese instruction states:

- the output form: choose, explain, rewrite, write sentences, or write a paragraph;
- the size: number of sentences or word range;
- every required idea or relationship;
- restrictions that genuinely matter, such as preserving meaning;
- the English topic/source material needed to answer.

There is no separate learner-facing “评分要点” box. Structured internal criteria are allowed only to mirror the instruction for consistent evaluation. If a criterion introduces a new required idea, the question is invalid.

## Clarity tests

Reject a question if:

- a learner must infer what “complete”, “natural”, “developed”, or “use the target” means;
- it asks for a second sentence, example, mechanism, concession, or restriction that appears only in marking data;
- a repair task omits the exact flawed text;
- two questions use substantially the same prompt and context;
- a selection question has more than one defensible answer under its wording;
- the word range cannot accommodate the required content;
- it uses backend vocabulary or internal skill labels.

## Marking after submission

Passed question: status only, collapsed.

Below-standard question:

1. quote the relevant learner text;
2. say what is missing or incorrect without an internal criterion name;
3. explain why it matters;
4. give one improved answer;
5. give one short next action.

The evaluator must accept different valid wording. Exact-string matching is limited to genuinely closed choices.
