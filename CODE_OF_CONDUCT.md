# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in IELTS Writing Coach a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Positive behavior includes:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes; and
- focusing on what is best for the community and its learners.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery and unwanted sexual attention or advances;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission; and
- conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Do not post a learner's essay, identity, credentials, account data, or other private material as part of a dispute or report.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, project chat, release events, and private communication about project work. It also applies when a person officially represents the project in public.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code, and will explain moderation decisions when doing so is appropriate and safe.

## Reporting

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the project owner, [@KevinYe0725](https://github.com/KevinYe0725), using a private contact method listed on that GitHub profile. If no private maintainer channel is available or the project owner is involved, use [GitHub's private abuse-reporting process](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

Do not put sensitive details in a public issue. If the incident also exposes a security vulnerability or private learner data, use the private process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters as far as reasonably possible. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is itself a violation.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will consider context, impact, and patterns of behavior when selecting a response:

1. **Correction** — a private or public explanation of why behavior was inappropriate, with a request to correct it.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning for a single incident or series of actions, with conditions for continued participation.
3. **Temporary restriction** — a temporary ban from interaction or contribution.
4. **Permanent restriction** — permanent removal from project participation for sustained violations, harassment, or aggression.
